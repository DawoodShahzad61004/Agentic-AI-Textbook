The difference is not actually the temperature value (0.1 vs 0.0). That would not cause a 404.

The important difference is this line in Snippet 2:

_os.environ.pop("GROQ_API_BASE", None)

That line removes a custom environment variable before creating the ChatGroq client.

What the error tells us

Your error is:

404 - Unknown request URL:
POST /openai/v1/openai/v1/chat/completions

Notice the duplicated path:

/openai/v1/openai/v1/chat/completions
          ↑ repeated

A normal Groq OpenAI-compatible endpoint would look like:

/openai/v1/chat/completions

not

/openai/v1/openai/v1/chat/completions

This strongly suggests that somewhere in your environment you have:

GROQ_API_BASE=https://api.groq.com/openai/v1

or something similar.

Then LangChain/Groq internally appends:

/openai/v1/chat/completions

resulting in:

https://api.groq.com/openai/v1/openai/v1/chat/completions

which produces the 404.

Why Snippet 2 works

Snippet 2 does:

load_dotenv()

_os.environ.pop("GROQ_API_BASE", None)

_llm = ChatGroq(...)

This removes the problematic variable.

After removal, ChatGroq falls back to its default endpoint:

https://api.groq.com/openai/v1/chat/completions

which is valid.

Snippet 1:
