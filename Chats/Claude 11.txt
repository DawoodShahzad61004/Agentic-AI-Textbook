The judge rules are carefully worded to match the style of the existing validators: paraphrase = present, structural differences ≠ facts, only query-relevant facts count as "lost."

agent_query.py — three additions
1. LBC_MIN_RETENTION_RATIO = 0.35
A module-level constant. If the compressed text is less than 35% the length of the original, the result is rejected before the judge is even called. This prevents runaway compression when the LLM strips too aggressively.
2. _LBC_COMPRESS_PROMPT + _LBC_DEFAULT_INSTRUCTIONS
The compressor prompt tells the LLM exactly what to do, with four explicit instruction lines:

Strip pure metadata (page numbers, titles, headers)
Strip document-structure sentences (e.g. "This section describes…")
Strip sentences that repeat facts already in other chunks

The __IRRELEVANT__ sentinel lets the LLM signal that an entire chunk contributes nothing to the query — those chunks are dropped entirely rather than kept as noise.
