Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) is a neurodevelopmental condition characterized by differences in social communication, restricted interests, repetitive behaviors, and sensory processing. The condition exists on a spectrum, meaning symptoms and support needs vary significantly between individuals [Source: ..\data\word\ASD_Patient_Management_Handbook.docx]. According to the Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders—5th edition (DSM-5), ASD is defined by deficits in social communication and the presence of restricted interests and repetitive behaviors [Source: ..\data\pdfs\Autism spectrum disorder definition, epidemiology, causes, and clinical evaluation.pdf].

The prevalence of ASD has been increasing over the years, with the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) reporting a prevalence of 1 in 54 children in the United States in 2022 [Source: ..\data\html\asd_disparities_gender_race_essay.html]. However, there are significant disparities in the diagnosis and treatment of ASD, particularly among girls and culturally diverse populations. These disparities are attributed to various factors, including limited access to screening tools and clinical training that reflect a broader range of autistic presentations [Source: ..\data\html\asd_disparities_gender_race_essay.html]. As a result, providers often overlook subtle social communication differences and masking behaviors in these populations.

Early identification and intervention are critical for improving outcomes for individuals with ASD. A comprehensive evaluation of an individual's behavior, communication, and developmental history is essential for accurate diagnosis [Source: ..\data\pdfs\Autism spectrum disorder definition, epidemiology, causes, and clinical evaluation.pdf]. Recommended treatments for ASD include Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA), Occupational Therapy (OT), Speech Therapy, and Social Skills Training [Source: learned_qa]. These therapies can help improve communication, social interaction, and daily living skills.

To reduce disparities among ASD patients, it is essential to address the systemic barriers that hinder access to diagnosis and treatment. This can be achieved by providing culturally responsive family communication, patient navigation support, and interpreters for families facing practical barriers [Source: ..\data\html\asd_disparities_gender_race_essay.html]. By promoting a more inclusive and supportive environment, we can improve outcomes for individuals with ASD and ensure that they receive the necessary support to thrive.
